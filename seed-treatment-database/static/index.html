About this tool, how to use it, and citation

What it is and who it is for

What’s on Your Seed? is an interactive product-identification and education resource for people who grow crops, advise farmers, teach, or work with seed treatments. Its purpose is to help users understand what is in a product and compare reported product facts.

How it works

The catalog uses EPA’s APPRIL Excel export to identify active registrations matching the seed-treatment sites listed in the crop filter. Records retain their full EPA registration numbers. Ingredient classifications and guidance come from separately identified references; saved Greenbook and CDMS references are distinguished from EPA data.

Known deltamethrin products and grain-storage-only entries are excluded. Crop groups are not expanded into unverified uses. Missing ingredient information is flagged for review. Federal registration does not establish state approval.

How to use it

  1. Search a product name, ingredient, or EPA number. Narrow results by crop or purpose; open More filters for technology, resistance groups, hazards, company, and dates.
  2. Read the ingredients and source notes. Open Details & label for the full product record, or follow its label link.
  3. Select Compare beside up to four products, then open Compare to view the complete matrix. Downloads and printing are grouped with the results.
  4. Open Guides & sources for abbreviations, ingredient information, treatment types, signal words, warnings, and source details.

Abbreviations & terms

Full names and plain-language explanations for abbreviations used in product information, treatment guidance, and source references.

Ingredients & resistance

AI / a.i.
Active ingredient. The component responsible for a pesticide’s intended pest-control effect.
DMI
Demethylation inhibitor. A fungicide group that disrupts sterol production in fungi; FRAC group 3.
FRAC
Fungicide Resistance Action Committee. The organization behind the fungicide resistance-group codes shown in this tool.
IRAC
Insecticide Resistance Action Committee. The organization behind the insecticide mode-of-action codes shown in this tool.
MBC
Methyl benzimidazole carbamate. A fungicide group that interferes with fungal cell division; FRAC group 1.
MOA / MoA
Mode of action. How a pesticide affects a biological process in its target organism.
QoI
Quinone outside inhibitor. A fungicide group that interrupts fungal respiration. The ingredient guide identifies the applicable FRAC code.
SDHI
Succinate dehydrogenase inhibitor. A fungicide group that interrupts fungal respiration; FRAC group 7.

FRAC and IRAC use separate classification systems. Their codes describe resistance groups, not ratings of safety or effectiveness. References: FRAC code list (PDF, new tab) and IRAC classification (new tab).

Safety & handling

BMP / BMPs
Best management practice(s). Practices intended to improve handling and reduce unintended impacts.
IPM
Integrated pest management. An approach combining pest identification, monitoring, prevention, and appropriate control methods.
PPE
Personal protective equipment. Protective clothing and equipment; use the current label to identify what is required.
RUP
Restricted-use pesticide. Refers to restricted-use classification. The tool displays the EPA export’s reported flag; check the current label for requirements.
SDS
Safety data sheet. A document describing chemical hazards and protective measures. Read it alongside the pesticide label.

References: EPA’s IPM principles (new tab), EPA labeling terminology (new tab), and OSHA’s safety data sheet guide (new tab).

Organizations & data sources

APPRIL
Active Pesticide Product Registration Informational Listing. The EPA database whose Excel export supplies this catalog’s product records.
CDMS
Crop Data Management Systems. A provider of agricultural product information; saved references are identified separately from EPA records.
CPN
Crop Protection Network. The agricultural education network associated with this tool.
DP
Distributor product. An EPA registration-type code in the source export. Keep the full distributor registration number when identifying a product.
EPA
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. The federal agency that supplies the registration data used here.
PPLS
Pesticide Product Label System. EPA’s searchable collection of pesticide product labels.
Sec3
Section 3 product. EPA’s registration-type code for a product registered under section 3 of the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act.

Reference: EPA database names and registration codes (new tab).
