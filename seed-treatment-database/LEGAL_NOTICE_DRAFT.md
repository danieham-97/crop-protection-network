# Proposed disclaimer for “What’s on Your Seed?”

**Draft for CPN and participating institutions’ legal review — October 21, 2026.** This is proposed language, not an adopted CPN policy or a guarantee of immunity. Counsel should confirm the entities covered, applicable law, placement, and any assent requirements before public deployment. The repository preview labels this notice as a draft.

## Proposed full notice

“What’s on Your Seed?” is an educational reference for identifying and comparing seed treatment products. It is not legal advice, a product recommendation, or authorization to purchase, sell, apply, plant, or otherwise use a pesticide or treated seed.

Information is compiled from external sources and may contain errors, omissions, or outdated material. Registration status, labels, restrictions, product formulations, and availability can change after a source is published or retrieved. Displayed dates describe the identified records; they do not guarantee that every field is current. A missing record does not establish that a product is prohibited, and a listed product does not establish that a particular use is permitted. Third-party information and linked websites are outside CPN’s control.

Before acting, users are responsible for independently confirming the exact product and full EPA registration number, current labeling and seed-tag instructions, federal and state registration status, and all applicable use restrictions and requirements with the appropriate regulatory authority and product manufacturer. Always read and follow current labeling. This tool does not replace those requirements or professional advice appropriate to the situation.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Crop Protection Network (CPN), its participating institutions, contributors, and data managers provide this tool and its information “as is” and “as available,” without express or implied warranties, including warranties of accuracy, completeness, timeliness, merchantability, or fitness for a particular purpose. To that same extent, they disclaim liability for losses, damages, claims, or expenses arising from use of, inability to use, or reliance on the tool, including errors, omissions, delayed updates, or inaccurate third-party information, and resulting crop injury, reduced yield, or economic loss. Nothing in this notice excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Reference to a product, manufacturer, or service does not constitute endorsement. Inclusion or omission is not a finding of safety, efficacy, suitability, or legal authorization.

## Short notice displayed with the tool

Educational reference only. Data may be incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date. Verify the exact product, current label, federal and state registration, and applicable restrictions before use. See the draft disclaimer and limitation of liability below.
